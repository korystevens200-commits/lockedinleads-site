The service

LockedinLeads provides automated lead response for service businesses. When someone enquires with a business using LockedinLeads, our system replies on that business's behalf, asks qualifying questions, and helps schedule an appointment.

SMS programme

By giving us your mobile number — through our website form, our web chat, or by texting or calling a number we operate — you agree to receive text messages from LockedinLeads relating to your enquiry.

  • What we send: replies to your enquiry, answers to your questions, appointment confirmations, appointment reminders, and occasional follow-ups if you have not replied.
  • How often: message frequency varies and depends on your conversation with us. Most enquiries involve a handful of messages.
  • Cost: we do not charge for messages. Message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier.
  • Carriers: mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP at any time and we will stop immediately and permanently. STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, OPT OUT, REMOVE ME and DO NOT CONTACT are all honoured the same way. There is no setting, exception or override anywhere in our system that can send to a number that has opted out.

If you change your mind, reply START or UNSTOP yourself — we cannot re-subscribe you on your behalf.

Getting help

Reply HELP for assistance, or korystevens200@gmail.com.

Quiet hours and frequency limits

Promotional messages are not sent between 9pm and 8am in your local time, and are limited to three per person per week. Replies to a message you sent us are never delayed by these limits.

Automated messages

Our replies are generated by an automated assistant. It identifies itself as an assistant rather than a person. It answers only from information the business has given it, and hands the conversation to a human when it is not confident.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-08-27

What we collect

When you enquire with a business that uses LockedinLeads, or with LockedinLeads directly, we collect the details you give us: your name, phone number, email address, and what you have told us about the job or enquiry. We also keep a record of the messages exchanged.

What we do with it

We use it to reply to you, answer your questions, schedule and remind you about appointments, and to give the business you contacted a record of your enquiry. That is all.

Mobile information

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes. Information you share with us stays between you and the business you contacted. We use service providers to deliver messages on our behalf — they may only process your information to provide that service, and may not use it for anything else.

Who can see your enquiry

The business you contacted, and LockedinLeads staff who operate the system for them. Each business's data is kept separate from every other business on the platform.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as the business needs them to serve you and to meet its own record-keeping obligations. You may ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices
